## Common Medical Abbreviations
- RMO: Resident Medical Officer
- INJ: Injection
- NS: Normal Saline
- IV: Intravenous
- OT: Operation Theatre
- ICU: Intensive Care Unit
- MRD: Medical Record Department

## Package vs Itemised Billing
Some hospitals charge a flat 'package' rate for surgeries, while others provide 'itemised' bills listing every single component. Insurers typically require itemised bills for auditing.
