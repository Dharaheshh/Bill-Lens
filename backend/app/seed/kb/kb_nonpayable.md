## Consumables
Consumables like gloves, masks, and syringes are typically excluded from insurance coverage because they are considered part of the hospital's operational overhead.

## Toiletries
Hospital-provided toiletries such as soap, toothbrushes, and admission kits are usually categorized as non-payable personal comfort items.

## Registration Fees
Registration and admission fees are often not covered as they are administrative charges rather than direct medical expenses.
