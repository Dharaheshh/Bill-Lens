## Room Rent Limits
Policies often cap the daily room rent. If you choose a room that costs more than this limit, you must pay the difference out of pocket.

## Proportionate Deduction
If the room rent exceeds the cap, other room-linked charges (like nursing, surgeon fees, and OT charges) are often reduced by the same proportion.
For example, if your cap is ₹5000 and you stay in a ₹10000 room (ratio 0.5), a surgeon fee of ₹40000 might only be reimbursed up to ₹20000.
